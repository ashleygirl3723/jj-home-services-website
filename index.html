Local, family-run, and built on helping people

J & J Home Services is a new, locally owned business based in Sweet Home, Oregon. It was started with a simple idea: people — especially seniors, folks with disabilities, and busy families — deserve dependable help around the house at a price that’s fair.

The owner brings more than 14 years of professional caregiving experience to every job. That background shows up in the work: patience, respect for your home and your privacy, and real attention to the small things that make daily life easier.

The long-term goal is a family business — something built to last in this community, and to one day grow into a business a son can step into. When you hire J & J, you’re hiring a neighbor who plans to be here.
